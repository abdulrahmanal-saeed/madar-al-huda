# Brand system — مدار الهدى | Madar Al-Huda

**P00-03 name and original icon approved by owner 2026-11-22.** The updated design uses its eight-point star and orbit on the Flutter startup screen and Android launcher. Legal trademark/store checks remain separate [R-10](../plan/RISKS_AND_BLOCKERS.md) before public release. See [source assets](../design/brand/README.md) and [clearance register](BRAND_CLEARANCE.md).

- AR: **مدار الهدى** · EN: **Madar Al-Huda**
- Tagline AR: **القرآن والعلم والعبادة في مكان واحد**
- Tagline EN: **Quran, Knowledge & Worship in One Place**
- Android package candidate `com.abdulrahmanalsaeed.madaralhuda` (O-08 not signed off), dev channel app-id suffix `.dev`.
- Colors: deep green `#0F4A43`, muted gold `#B8893A`, ivory `#F5F0E6`, dark ink `#12332F`. Readable gold text uses `#6E5420` on ivory.
- Icon: eight-point star with an open orbit and a single orbiting dot, Android adaptive foreground and monochrome sources, SVG and PNG legacy raster density exports.
- Typography: **Reem Kufi** for the wordmark and headings, **Amiri Quran** for Quran text, **Amiri** for hadith text and the dedication, and **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** for the Arabic and English interface. Font files and their OFL notices are bundled. [Reem Kufi 3.0](https://github.com/aliftype/reem-kufi/releases/tag/v3.0) · [Amiri 1.003](https://github.com/aliftype/amiri/releases/tag/1.003) · [IBM Plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex)
- Layout: full RTL/LTR, system dark/light, ≥48dp targets, 2× text scaling, WCAG AA contrast, reduced motion; logo mark itself does not mirror.
- Brand final review: verify icon/wordmarks in small sizes, light/dark, Arabic/English, store availability and professional legal opinion prior to public release.
