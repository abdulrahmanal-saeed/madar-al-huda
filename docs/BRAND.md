# Brand system — مدار الهدى | Madar Al-Huda

**Current P00-03 name/logo approved by owner 2026-11-22. No redesign.** Legal trademark/store checks remain separate [R-10](../plan/RISKS_AND_BLOCKERS.md) before public release. See [source assets](../design/brand/README.md) and [clearance register](BRAND_CLEARANCE.md). No public trademark is claimed.

- AR: **مدار الهدى** · EN: **Madar Al-Huda**
- Tagline AR: **القرآن والعلم والعبادة في مكان واحد**
- Tagline EN: **Quran, Knowledge & Worship in One Place**
- Android package candidate `com.abdulrahmanalsaeed.madaralhuda` (O-08 not signed off), dev channel app-id suffix `.dev`.
- Colors: deep green `#173F39`, muted gold `#C7A86D`, ivory `#F8F5ED`, dark ink `#263B38`. Readable gold text uses `#6E5420` on ivory.
- Icon: original open book and orbital ring (no holy text), Android adaptive foreground and monochrome sources, SVG and PNG legacy raster density exports.
- UI typography: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** for both AR and EN, licensed OFL-1.1 [official source](https://github.com/IBM/plex/blob/master/packages/plex-sans-arabic/LICENSE.txt); font binary already bundled with license. Quran typeface is a separate unapproved future task.
- Layout: full RTL/LTR, system dark/light, ≥48dp targets, 2× text scaling, WCAG AA contrast, reduced motion; logo mark itself does not mirror.
- Brand final review: verify icon/wordmarks in small sizes, light/dark, Arabic/English, store availability and professional legal opinion prior to public release.
